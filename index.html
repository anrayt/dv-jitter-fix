DV Jitter Fix

Drop clips recorded off a camcorder with a DVR. Every second frame in them jumps about a pixel up or down. This page finds that jump in each clip and lines the frames back up. Your videos never leave your device.

This browser can't process video. Open the page in Chrome or Edge on a computer, or Safari 17+ on Mac or iPhone.

Tapes

What it fixes

The camera sends interlaced video: each frame is two half-frames, odd lines and even lines. The DVR saves each half-frame as its own frame at 50 fps. The two halves sit about a pixel apart, so the picture shakes even on a tripod.

Output50 fpssame frames, H.264 .mp4, 24 Mbit/s

Example numbers measured on clips from a Sony DCR-PC115E. Frames are moved on the original YUV data with Lanczos interpolation, split between both frames of each pair so sharpness stays even.
